Malware and Cyber Threats

WannaCry Ransomware

WannaCry was a piece of ransomware that started spreading on May 12, 2017, locking files on infected Windows computers and demanding a Bitcoin payment to unlock them. It spread on its own through a Windows file-sharing flaw, hitting more than 200,000 computers in about 150 countries, including hospitals in the UK's National Health Service. Microsoft had already released a patch for the flaw two months earlier, so most of the damage landed on organizations that had not installed their updates. It matters because it showed that a simple missed update can shut down critical services like healthcare, which is why patching is a core part of managing information systems.


Types of Malware

  1. Ransomware - encrypts files and demands payment.
  2. Trojan horse - hides inside software that looks harmless.

Common Hacker Motives

  1. Financial gain through ransom or stolen data.
  2. Espionage against companies or governments.

Prevention Strategies

  • Install software updates and security patches quickly.
  • Use multi-factor authentication and strong, unique passwords.
